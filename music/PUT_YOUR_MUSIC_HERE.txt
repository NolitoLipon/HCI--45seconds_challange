BACKGROUND MUSIC (optional)
===========================
The Music button works WITHOUT any file: the page makes its own calm tune
that changes with each Breathing Style (Water is soft, Flame has drums, Thunder is fast,
Wisteria is dreamy).

To use your own music instead, save an MP3 here with this exact name:
    background.mp3
Then press the Music button (or the M key). It starts only when you press it.

Tip: use music you are allowed to use (your own, or free to use). You can delete this note.
